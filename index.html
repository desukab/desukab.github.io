Frequently Asked Questions

Common concerns from clients before starting a project.

I implement rotating proxies, user-agent rotation, and request throttling with configurable concurrency limits (e.g., asyncio.Semaphore). Every scraper includes exponential backoff retry logic and respects robots.txt. For Cloudflare-protected sites, I use Playwright stealth configurations to mimic real browser fingerprints.
Modern sites use Cloudflare, DataDome, PerimeterX, and similar services. I match them head-on with Playwright stealth mode (hiding headless browser flags), real browser fingerprinting, cookie persistence, and JavaScript challenge solving. For simpler protections, I use realistic headers, referrers, and behavioral patterns.
I deliver data in whatever format fits your workflow: CSV, JSON, Excel (.xlsx), SQLite databases, or even direct API endpoints. Most clients prefer CSV or Excel for immediate use, or SQLite when they need indexed, queryable storage. I can also push directly to Google Sheets or your CRM via API.
Every pipeline includes automated validation: schema enforcement, deduplication on unique IDs/URLs, missing-value imputation, regex cleansing of whitespace and broken characters, and type normalization (currency to float, dates to ISO-8601). I also generate an HTML summary report showing record counts, dropped duplicates, and data completeness percentages.
Simple static-site scrapers: 1-2 days. Complex anti-bot or dynamic site projects: 3-5 days. Full ETL pipelines with cleaning and database setup: 5-7 days. I always provide a sample output within 24 hours of starting so you can validate the data schema before the full run.
