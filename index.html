02 / THE IDEA

Editors should start with a story — not a mountain of footage.

Long recordings contain the useful moment somewhere inside hours of context, repetition and dead air. SelekyT is being built to do the painful first pass before the editor takes over.

THE PRODUCT

Raw footage → editorial decisions → editable timeline.

Instead of stopping at a transcript or a flattened auto-edit, SelekyT is designed around the thing editors actually need: a structured starting point they can review, change and continue editing.

AI PROPOSES. THE EDITOR DECIDES.
FOR CREATORS

Find the moments.

Surface stories and publishable moments without scrubbing through everything manually.

FOR EDITORS

Keep the control.

Start from an editorial sequence instead of surrendering the final cut to a black box.
